# Blades in the Dark — Character Playbooks & Progression

This document specifies the data structure, baseline abilities, item sets, and XP advancement rules for all 7 official Scoundrel playbooks.

---

## 1. Playbook Definitions

### 1. Cutter (The Fighter)
* **XP Trigger**: *Address a challenge with violence or coercion.*
* **Starting Actions**: +1 Skirmish, +1 Command (or player allocates 2 to playbook rating).
* **Special Abilities**:
  - `Battleborn`: May push yourself to gain +1d or +1 effect when fighting multiple opponents or an enemy of superior scale.
  - `Bodyguard`: When you protect a teammate, take +1d to your resistance roll. When you gather info to anticipate threats, take +1d.
  - `Ghost Fighter`: Can harm spirits and astral entities with hand-to-hand combat and weapons as if they were physical.
  - `Leader`: When leading a group action in combat, take 1 less stress if teammates roll 1-3.
  - `Mule`: Your load limits increase: Light = 5, Normal = 7, Heavy = 8.
  - `Not to be Trifled With`: Can push yourself to battle a large gang on equal footing or perform a feat of superhuman strength.
  - `Savage`: When unleashing violence, take +1 level of harm dealt or terror inspired.
  - `Vigorous`: Recover from harm faster; your healing project clocks advance with +1 tick.
* **Unique Items**: Fine hand weapon (1 load), Fine heavy weapon (2 load), Scary weapon or armor (2 load), Manacles & key (0 load), Rage essence vial (0 load).

### 2. Hound (The Tracker & Sniper)
* **XP Trigger**: *Address a challenge with tracking or violence.*
* **Starting Actions**: +1 Hunt, +1 Survey.
* **Special Abilities**:
  - `Sharpshooter`: Can push yourself to shoot beyond standard range or hit impossible targets without penalty.
  - `Focused`: Can resist consequences of distraction, illusion, or confusion with +1d.
  - `Ghost Hunter`: Hunting hound is attuned to spirits and can track ethereal presence.
  - `Scout`: When gathering info on enemy positions or patrol routes, take +1d and +1 effect.
  - `Survivor`: Suffer 1 less stress when rolling resistance using Resolve.
  - `Tough as Nails`: Penalties from Level 1 and Level 2 harm do not apply to you.
  - `Trained Hunting Pet`: Cohort companion with predatory edge and loyalty.
  - `Vengeful`: When taking harm from an enemy, mark +1 xp in an attribute track.
* **Unique Items**: Fine pair of pistols (1 load), Fine long rifle (2 load), Electroplasmic ammunition (1 load), Camouflage cloak (1 load), Hunting hound (cohort).

### 3. Leech (The Alchemist & Mechanist)
* **XP Trigger**: *Address a challenge with technical skill or mayhem.*
* **Starting Actions**: +1 Tinker, +1 Wreck.
* **Special Abilities**:
  - `Alchemist`: Can invent, craft, and modify concoctions, poisons, and oils with 1 fewer downtime roll.
  - `Analyst`: When gathering info via Study or Tinker, take +1d and +1 effect.
  - `Artificer`: Can craft mechanical apparatuses, clockwork, and traps with +1 tier quality.
  - `Fortitude`: Suffer 1 less stress when rolling resistance against poison, disease, or chemicals.
  - `Ghost Ward`: Can disperse spirits and disrupt spectral barriers using electroplasmic charges.
  - `Physicker`: Can treat wounds and heal companions without penalty (unlocks the Doctor role).
  - `Saboteur`: When wrecking structural targets or setting demolition charges, effect is Great.
  - `Venomous`: Immune to 1 chosen poison or chemical concoction.
* **Unique Items**: Fine tinker tools (1 load), Fine wrecking kit (2 load), Blowpipe & darts (0 load), Bandolier of alchemicals (1 load, contains 3 doses of oils/poisons).

### 4. Lurk (The Infiltrator & Burglar)
* **XP Trigger**: *Address a challenge with stealth or evasion.*
* **Starting Actions**: +1 Prowl, +1 Finesse.
* **Special Abilities**:
  - `Infiltrator`: Not affected by quality or tier penalties when bypassing physical security.
  - `Daredevil`: When rolling a desperate action, gain +1d to the roll (in addition to standard desperate XP).
  - `The Automobile / Ghost Veil`: Can slip into the shadow of the Ghost Field for silent travel.
  - `Ambush`: When attacking from unseen stealth, action roll has Great effect.
  - `Shadow`: Spend 1 stress to vanish from sight into an existing shadow.
  - `Reflexes`: When reacting to sudden ambushes or traps, take +1d to resistance.
  - `Tenacious`: Complete recovery or long-term infiltration project clocks with +1 tick.
  - `Expertise`: Can push yourself to reroll 1 die on any Prowl or Finesse roll.
* **Unique Items**: Fine lockpicks (0 load), Fine shadow cloak (1 load), Light climbing gear (1 load), Silence potion (0 load), Dark-sight goggles (1 load).

### 5. Slide (The Manipulator & Grifter)
* **XP Trigger**: *Address a challenge with deception or influence.*
* **Starting Actions**: +1 Sway, +1 Consort.
* **Special Abilities**:
  - `Rook's Gambit`: Spend 2 stress to roll your best action rating in place of any other action rating.
  - `Cloak & Dagger`: When using a disguise or alias, take +1d to avoid discovery.
  - `Ghost Voice`: Can speak to ghosts and spirits as if they were living people without fear or penalty.
  - `Like Looking in a Mirror`: You always know when someone is lying to you.
  - `A Little Something on the Side`: Gain +1 Coin whenever you successfully finish a score.
  - `Mesmerism`: When swaying a solitary target, they will believe whatever plausible lie you tell.
  - `Subterfuge`: Can push yourself to convince an enemy to turn on their allies for a critical turn.
  - `Trust in Me`: Allies who assist you gain +1d to their own next action.
* **Unique Items**: Fine clothes & disguise kit (1 load), Fine loaded dice & trick cards (0 load), Trance powder (0 load), Cane concealing a thin blade (1 load).

### 6. Spider (The Mastermind & Architect)
* **XP Trigger**: *Address a challenge with calculation or conspiracy.*
* **Starting Actions**: +1 Consort, +1 Study.
* **Special Abilities**:
  - `Foresight`: Two times per score, take a flashback with a stress cost discount of 1 (minimum 0).
  - `Calculating`: Suffer 1 less stress when resisting consequences with Insight.
  - `Connected`: When acquiring an asset or gathering info in downtime, take +1d.
  - `Functioning Vice`: When indulging your vice, you may also clear 1 stress from an ally.
  - `Mastermind`: Can lead a group action using Consort or Command even if separated from teammates.
  - `Weaving the Web`: Gain +1 Rep whenever a score goes off without an alarm or alert.
  - `Jail Bird`: When incarcerated, your Wanted Level does not hinder your faction operations.
  - `Ghost Contract`: Binding agreements backed by electroplasmic oath.
* **Unique Items**: Fine blueprints & dossier (1 load), Fine fountain pen & sealed documents (0 load), Pocket watch (0 load), Concealed poison ring (0 load).

### 7. Whisper (The Arcane Adept)
* **XP Trigger**: *Address a challenge with arcane power or the supernatural.*
* **Starting Actions**: +1 Attune, +1 Study.
* **Special Abilities**:
  - `Compel`: Attune to force a captured or present spirit to obey an instruction.
  - `Ghost Mind`: Suffer 1 less stress when resisting supernatural fear or possession.
  - `Iron Will`: Immune to the terror of seeing spirits or horrors.
  - `Occultist`: Can craft esoteric charms, talismans, and soul jars.
  - `Ritual`: Can perform arcane rituals to manifest impossible magical effects.
  - `Strange Methods`: Can push yourself to glimpse a future or alternate timeline.
  - `Tempest`: Can summon electroplasmic lightning, cold winds, or static shocks.
  - `Warded`: Possess an innate ward that absorbs 1 supernatural harm per score.
* **Unique Items**: Fine lightning hook (2 load), Fine spirit mask (1 load), Ghost key (0 load), Spirit bottles (1 load), Arcane talismans (0 load).

## 3. Experience & Advancement Engine

### XP Tracks
1. **Playbook Track** (8 XP boxes): Unlocks 1 new Special Ability or an action dot in any rating.
2. **Insight Track** (6 XP boxes): Unlocks 1 action dot in Hunt, Study, Survey, or Tinker.
3. **Prowess Track** (6 XP boxes): Unlocks 1 action dot in Finesse, Prowl, Skirmish, or Wreck.
4. **Resolve Track** (6 XP boxes): Unlocks 1 action dot in Attune, Command, Consort, or Sway.

### End of Session XP Triggers
Every player answers 3 end-of-session trigger categories:
1. Did you address a challenge with your playbook's key approach? (Mark 1 or 2 XP).
2. Did you express your beliefs, drives, heritage, or background? (Mark 1 or 2 XP).
3. Did you struggle with issues from your vice or traumas? (Mark 1 or 2 XP).
*Player allocates marked session XP to their Playbook track or any Attribute track.*

## 4. Playbook Contacts & Street Relations Engine

### Character Creation Invariant (Strict Rule)
Every Scoundrel at creation must select:
* **1 Close Friend** (▲): Marked contact initializes relationship score to **85**.
* **1 Rival / Foe** (▼): Marked contact initializes relationship score to **15**.
* **Neutral Contacts**: Other playbook or street contacts default to **50**.

### 1–100 Relationship Sentiment Scale
* **1–20**: Bitter Foe / Arch-Enemy
* **21–40**: Rival / Adversary
* **41–60**: Neutral / Acquaintance
* **61–80**: Ally / Friendly Contact
* **81–100**: Devoted Friend / Close Confidant

### Badges & Labels System
* **Systemwide Badges**: `Mentor`, `Protégé`, `Debtor`, `Creditor`, `Lover`, `Family`, `Blackmailer`, `Former Partner`, `Confidant`.
* **Campaign-Exclusive Labels**: Dynamically created per campaign to track unique faction compacts, blood oaths, and score debts.
* **Faction Affiliation**: Each contact/NPC links directly to a campaign Faction (`faction_id`) with a specified role or title within that organization, or can be designated Independent.
